Who We Are

Building on Solid Ground Since 1981

Mobrin Solutions is a limited company whose main stay includes Foundation Design and Piling, Geotechnical Investigation and Civil Engineering Material Laboratories. In addition to these services, the company consults in Quality Assurance for construction materials.

The services and products we offer are specifically tailored to meet our customers' needs and expectations. Our highly skilled and dedicated team of professionals guarantee viable solutions to your business enquiries — backed by a customer-focused approach, multi-disciplined resources and modern technology.

  • Contractors & Construction Managers since 1981
  • Head Office in Kitwe, Branch Office in Lusaka
  • Modern equipment sourced from BL Herbert International LLC, USA
