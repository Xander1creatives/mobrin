Our Story

Contractors & Construction Managers Since 1981

Mobrin Solutions is a limited company whose main stay includes Foundation Design and Piling, Geotechnical Investigation and Civil Engineering Material Laboratories. In addition to these services, the company consults in Quality Assurance for construction materials.

The services and products we offer are specifically tailored to meet our customers' needs and expectations. Our highly skilled and dedicated team of professionals guarantee viable solutions to your business enquiries.

This customer-focused approach, coupled with multi-disciplined resources and the availability of modern technology, ensures that our valued clients are satisfied with the services and products we provide.
