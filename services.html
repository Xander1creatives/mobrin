Geotechnical Engineering Services

Mobrin Solutions is able to offer complete Geotechnical Services covering the full lifecycle of a project — from initial planning through to foundation design.

  • Planning of the investigation, tailored to project requirements and site conditions
  • Execution of field work (boreholes, sampling, in-situ tests) and laboratory testing
  • Interpretation, reporting and practical recommendations for safe, cost-effective designs
  • Piling and foundation design based on geotechnical findings and project needs
Request a Site Visit

Site Investigation & Laboratory Testing

We provide design data for structural works, roads, dams and all other Building & Civil Engineering Projects, through Field & Site Investigation and Laboratory Testing services.

  • Excavation of trial pits & soft/rock ground drilling
  • Continuous Dynamic Penetration Test & Standard Penetration Test
  • In-situ density testing, bulk sampling, digital site photography & GIS mapping
  • Laboratory testing for soils, concrete aggregates & mixes, bricks & blocks, bituminous materials

Concrete Quality Assurance

Through newly acquired modern equipment from BL Herbert International LLC, an American firm — used on the New Embassy Compound project in Ibex, Lusaka — we can formulate a Concrete Quality Assurance Program to suit the needs of a specific site.

  • Check on suitability of concrete materials
  • Testing of fresh and hardened concrete
  • Concrete cube tests & concrete cylinder crushing
  • Schmidt hammer tests
